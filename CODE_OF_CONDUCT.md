# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want iLoathePDF to be a friendly, harassment-free place to ask questions,
report bugs and contribute, whatever your experience level, background or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith, and give people the benefit of the doubt.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique the work, not the
  person.
- Keep discussion on topic and be mindful that many people here are volunteers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in any form.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory remarks.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as their documents or
  contact details, without their permission.
- Spam, or advertising unrelated to the project.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose
behaviour is a problem.

To report a violation, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/Nisarg6502/iLoathePDF/security/advisories/new)
and say it is a Code of Conduct report. Reports are handled privately.
